ABOUT ENTERSEED

Enterprise meets
a new beginning.

Enterseed brings enterprise support and the promise of a seed together, with a name that also echoes the idea of interceding: helping close the distance between a business owner and the support they need.

Our point of view

Grow Your Business,
Together.

A good business idea deserves a clearer path from first question to next opportunity.

Enterseed is designed around the realities of micro, small, and medium enterprises in South Cotabato. We bring plain-language registration guidance, planning tools, and local connections into one approachable starting point.

Our pilot focus includes entrepreneurs in Koronadal City, Polomolok, and nearby SOCCSKSARGEN communities, including unregistered founders preparing to begin and registered businesses ready to expand.
