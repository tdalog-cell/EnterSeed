Why we’re building this

One place to
get started.

Starting or growing a business is easier when the next step is clear.

Enterseed is being built for micro, small, and medium businesses in South Cotabato. It brings registration guidance, planning tools, and local supplier information together.

We’re starting with business owners in Koronadal City, Polomolok, and nearby SOCCSKSARGEN communities. The tools are for people preparing to register as well as established businesses looking to grow.
